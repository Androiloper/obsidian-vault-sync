Privacy policy

Obsidian Vault Sync. Last updated 7 October 2026.

Who this applies to

Obsidian Vault Sync is a personal tool used only by the person who built it, with that person's own Google account. Nobody else is invited to use it.

What data it accesses

The plugin reads and writes the note files in the owner's Obsidian vaults, and the copies of those files that it stores in the owner's own Google Drive. Its Google permission is limited to files and folders the plugin itself created (the drive.file scope). It has no access to any other Drive content, to email, to contacts, or to any other Google data.

Where data goes

Files travel directly between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive. There is no server operated by the developer, and no data is sent anywhere else. Nothing is collected, analysed, sold, or shared with any third party.

What is stored on the device

The Google sign-in token and a sync index are stored locally inside the Obsidian vault's plugin folder and the app's local storage on each device. Removing the plugin, or using its Disconnect button, deletes the sign-in from that device.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access page. Files already synced stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them.

Use of Google user data

The plugin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
